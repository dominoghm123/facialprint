CONCEPT

FACIAL PRINT is a real-time biometric visualization system that translates facial geometry, expression, and movement into a living particle portrait. The face is presented not as image — but as data field.

CORE MECHANICS

GEOMETRY → PARTICLES
468 FaceMesh landmarks seed a particle cloud

DEPTH → DENSITY
Shadowed areas attract denser clusters

EXPRESSION → COLOR
Smile → warm. Tension → cool. Neutral → white

MOTION → SOUND
Head rotation modulates ambient audio field
